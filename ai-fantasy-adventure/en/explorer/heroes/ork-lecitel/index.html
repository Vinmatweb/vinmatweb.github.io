Starting hero · Level 1

Orc – Healer

Mighty strength guided by a courageous heart. Keeps the party standing and drives back the dark.

Orcs are the strongest of the core races. When their Health is low, their close-range attacks grow stronger, though magic is harder for them. Healers combine Intelligence and Charisma, begin with four spells, and have the strongest healing modifier. They can use first aid outside their magic limit.
