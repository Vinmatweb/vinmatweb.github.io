Examples for parents and Vaelor

Three adventures for about 15 minutes

Each story has five short scenes. Vaelor can adapt pace, challenge, and tone to the children; the scene order is a guide, not a fixed script.

3 × 5 scenes

Old forest · discovery and teamwork

The Bell Beneath the Roots

Main goal

Find the silver bell that protects a forest spring.

Obstacles

A picture puzzle and a wary forest creature; combat is unnecessary.

Scene outline

  1. Tiny glowing tracks appear on the path, followed by a faint chime.
  2. Roots cover the spring; three stones show water, leaf, and sun symbols.
  3. A small forest creature has the bell and is frightened by the noisy woods.
  4. The heroes can reassure it, offer help, or suggest another safe plan.
  5. When the bell returns, the spring rings again and the group earns a peaceful thank-you.
Possible twist

The creature took the bell to call for help for an injured friend.

Prompt for Vaelor / Oryn

Guide a friendly adventure called “The Bell Beneath the Roots” for children, lasting about fifteen minutes. Use this goal: Find the silver bell that protects a forest spring. Prepare five scenes from the outline, while letting players suggest their own solutions. Describe only the current scene, offer 2–3 possibilities, then ask what they do. Do not force combat; if they fail, move the story forward. Follow the uploaded Manual and do not invent new statistics or rewards.

Mountain trail · planning and choices

The Bridge in the Mist

Main goal

Carry a bundle of healing herbs safely across a mountain stream.

Obstacles

Time pressure, mist, and a safe crossing; every plan has a trade-off.

Scene outline

  1. Mist hides the fork in the trail; the group finds an old map with one missing symbol.
  2. A wooden bridge is crooked; the heroes inspect its ropes, planks, and the safe bank.
  3. A lost courier calls from the far side and knows a shorter route.
  4. The group chooses to repair the bridge, find a ford, or help the courier first.
  5. The herbs arrive in time; Vaelor recaps how the group’s choice helped the mountain village.
Possible twist

The map is not wrong—the bridge shifted after the spring flood.

Village at dusk · clues and conversation

The Lantern on the Green

Main goal

Find the lantern that guides travellers safely through the square each night.

Obstacles

Connect clues and talk to people; nobody has to be blamed or defeated.

Scene outline

  1. Villagers prepare a festival, but the lantern is missing and night is near.
  2. Witnesses recall different clues: wet footprints, a smell of honey, and rustling by the barn.
  3. The heroes follow clues and can speak with a merchant, a child, or the night watch.
  4. In a shed they find the lantern and a sleepy creature hiding from the loud festival.
  5. The group decides how to return the lantern and help the creature; the festival can begin.
Possible twist

The creature mistook the warm lantern for a safe place and borrowed it to stay warm.
