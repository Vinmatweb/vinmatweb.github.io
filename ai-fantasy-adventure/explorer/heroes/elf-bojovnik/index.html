Elf – Warrior

An elf warrior combines attentiveness and a keen mind with a willingness to face danger directly. The character may protect a forest road, accompany a messenger, or leave home because of an unfulfilled promise. Within the party, the warrior serves as a defender who watches the surroundings and seeks a good position. A thoughtful approach can complement brute strength, but does not automatically grant an extra special strike.

Within the party, the warrior helps keep danger away from more vulnerable allies and gives them room to make decisions. The character may guard a retreat, accompany a negotiator, or hold an important passage. The role is useful outside combat too: protecting travelers, preparing an expedition, and handling situations where someone needs support. Courage may mean admitting a mistake or choosing a safer route, rather than accepting every challenge.

The racial ability Keen Sight automatically reveals a distant visible detail, tracks, or movement unless they are magically or objectively hidden. The class adds Protect: once per battle, the warrior may take an attack directed at a nearby ally. The attack is resolved against the warrior's defense instead. Choose starting equipment according to the requirements this particular hero meets. The listed values form the basis of a level-one character; personality, name, relationships, and the reason for adventuring are decided during character creation.
