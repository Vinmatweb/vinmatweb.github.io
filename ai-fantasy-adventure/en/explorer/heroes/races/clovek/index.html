Playable race

Human

A hero who chooses their own path.

Humans are adaptable adventurers with no predetermined strong or weak attribute. During character creation, the player chooses one of each, and they must be different.

Racial ability

Adaptability

Once per adventure, after a failed roll of their own, the human may try again. Vaelor creates a new random permutation, the player chooses a number again, and the better result is used.
