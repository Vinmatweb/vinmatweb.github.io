Main project · Free printables

VinMat’s World for Kids

Find coloring pages, mazes, dot-to-dots and tracing worksheets by age, difficulty and theme. Open a preview, choose an available version, then download or print the activity for home or classroom use. Easier pages suit first attempts; detailed designs offer a longer challenge.

The activity guides explain how to choose a level, prepare a printout and help children get started. Materials are free for personal and educational use, with no account needed.

Fantasy RPG · AI Dungeon Master

AI Fantasy Adventure

Explore Elaria through a shared fantasy story. Download the game documents, choose a hero and use a separate AI chat as your Game Master. Players describe their choices while Vaelor, the AI guide, develops the adventure using the game rules.

The world guide connects heroes, creatures, equipment, spells and rules. An adult can prepare a game, find a suitable character and look up details during play. The website itself does not provide an AI chat.

Printable learning games

VM Edu Games

Turn addition, subtraction and number order into a tabletop game. Print the cards, prepare a small starting set and let each calculation change a fantasy battle or a car race. Choose a simpler setup first and increase the number range when the players are ready.

VM Fantasy Battle and VM Racing Challenge include printable components and rules. Their pages explain the learning goal, preparation and available versions so you can choose what to print before you start.
