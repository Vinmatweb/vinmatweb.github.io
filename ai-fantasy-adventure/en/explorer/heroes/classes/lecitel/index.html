Character class

Healer

Keeps the party standing and drives back the dark.

Healers combine Intelligence and Charisma, begin with four spells, and have the strongest healing modifier. They can use first aid outside their magic limit.

Healing, protection, cleansing, support, and calm leadership.

Class ability

First Aid

Once per combat, restores ⌈Intelligence/2⌉ Health to themself or one ally, up to the maximum. This is not a healing spell and does not use the healing magic limit.
