The world of AI Fantasy Adventure

Wonder and danger

Elaria is a magical land of great stories, where wonders and dangers wait beyond the safe roads. Courage alone is not enough to survive: heroes need strength, good judgment, trusted companions, and magic used wisely.

Forests full of secrets

Old paths wind among ruins, hidden springs, and places where magic has left its mark.

Journeys that test the party

In the mountains, preparation, courage, and a protective spell can decide whether everyone returns safely.

For parents · first game

Choose a simple hero and start with one clear goal

  1. Choose a hero based on what the child enjoys; suggested race and class combinations are ideas, not restrictions.
  2. Tell Vaelor about the group, the children’s ages, and the tone they want. Upload the game documents beforehand.
  3. Guide 5–8 scenes: introduce a goal, offer choices and obstacles, then wrap up. Children decide what their heroes try.
