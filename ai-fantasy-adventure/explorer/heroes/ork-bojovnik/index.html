Orc – Warrior

An orc warrior excels in strength and the courage to face threats at close quarters. The character's past may involve defending a family, serving in a guard, or trying to put right a leader's mistake. Appearance does not determine personality: the warrior may be a patient protector, a cheerful companion, or someone still learning to control anger. Reliable allies matter as much to this character as to any other hero.

Within the party, the warrior helps keep danger away from more vulnerable allies and gives them room to make decisions. The character may guard a retreat, accompany a negotiator, or hold an important passage. The role is useful outside combat too: protecting travelers, preparing an expedition, and handling situations where someone needs support. Courage may mean admitting a mistake or choosing a safer route, rather than accepting every challenge.

The racial ability Battle Fury grants +1 to the result of every melee attack while the character is below half of maximum HP. The class adds Protect: once per battle, the warrior may take an attack directed at a nearby ally. The attack is resolved against the warrior's defense instead. Choose starting equipment according to the requirements this particular hero meets. The listed values form the basis of a level-one character; personality, name, relationships, and the reason for adventuring are decided during character creation.
