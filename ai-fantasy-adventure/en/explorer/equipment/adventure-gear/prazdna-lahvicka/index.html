Empty vial

An empty vial is an ordinary container for a small amount of liquid. In Elaria, it may be carried by a researcher, healer, craftsperson, or curious adventurer. A mark on the glass, its shape, and a small inscription can identify its origins without giving the item itself a magical ability. Sometimes an empty container is a reminder of what it once held.

It is useful for keeping an ordinary liquid and clearly describing a discovery. It does not automatically turn its contents into a potion or guarantee their safety or the preservation of a magical effect. The party may want to take a sample for someone who understands it. Identifying the contents and using them are separate questions that must follow from the story and the rules.

Rules effect: Holding a small amount of ordinary liquid. The price is 5 cp. The item is reusable rather than a single-use supply. Its listed effect applies to the situation it is intended for. Additional bonuses, other forms of protection, and new magical abilities are not part of this entry.
