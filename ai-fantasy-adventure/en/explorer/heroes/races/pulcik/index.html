Playable race

Halfling

Small in stature, lucky, and quick with their hands.

Halflings rely on Agility and remarkable Luck. They are not strong, but once per adventure can turn a failure into a narrow success.

Racial ability

Lucky Break

Once per adventure, after their own failed virtual roll, changes the result to a narrow success. The roll is not repeated; this is the lowest success needed, with no extra benefit.
