Fear

On a success, an ordinary target cannot approach the caster and gets −2 to its next attack. A boss can still move, but keeps the attack penalty.

Minimum INT 7 · Level —+0 to MA→
Control

Weaken

On a success, the target gets −2 to Physical Attack (PA). It does not affect Magic Attack (MA) or defenses.

Minimum INT 8 · Level 2+1 to MA→
Control

Curse

On a success, choose STR, AGI, INT, CHA, or Luck. The target gets −2 to its next two checks using that attribute. This penalty does not stack.

Minimum INT 8 · Level 2+1 to MA→
Control

Dark Mist

Attacks made from or into the mist suffer −2 to their final PA or MA. A creature inside cannot see outside it; the penalty applies to everyone.

Offensive

Drain Life

After calculating damage normally, the caster restores as much Health as the lower of 2 or the damage actually dealt. This extra Health does not use the healing multiplier.

Minimum INT 9 · Level 3+4 to MA→
Control

Ghostly Shackles

On a success, an ordinary target cannot move but can still act within reach. A boss has only half movement instead.

Creates a nonattacking skeletal servant. It can carry up to 20 kg and follow simple commands such as fetch, hold, open, or guard; it cannot fight or speak.
