Starting hero · Level 1

Human – Bard

A hero who chooses their own path. Stories, music, and the right words at the right moment.

Humans are adaptable adventurers with no predetermined strong or weak attribute. During character creation, the player chooses one of each, and they must be different. Bards rely on Charisma and Agility. Once in an important scene or combat, they can improve their own or an ally's roll bonus by one step.

Racial ability · Human

Adaptability

Once per adventure, after a failed roll of their own, the human may try again. Vaelor creates a new random permutation, the player chooses a number again, and the better result is used.

Class ability · Bard

Inspiration

Once during a combat or important scene, after a virtual roll is revealed, may raise their own or an ally's roll bonus by one step, up to that category's maximum. It takes no main action and cannot be used on an enemy.
