Character class

Bard

Stories, music, and the right words at the right moment.

Bards rely on Charisma and Agility. Once in an important scene or combat, they can improve their own or an ally's roll bonus by one step.

Social scenes, party support, music, and versatility.

Class ability

Inspiration

Once during a combat or important scene, after a virtual roll is revealed, may raise their own or an ally's roll bonus by one step, up to that category's maximum. It takes no main action and cannot be used on an enemy.

Starting loadout

Active equipment

  • Short Sword
  • Dagger
  • Leather Armor
  • One musical instrument of choice

In the inventory

Backpack · Flint and steel · Waterskin · Trail rations

You may choose a crossbow or another suitable weapon if you meet its requirements and Vaelor keeps the loadout at roughly the same value.
