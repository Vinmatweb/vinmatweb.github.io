Fairy – Warrior

A tiny magical being with healing dust and light wings. Holds the front line and protects the party.

14 Health

Fairies are small winged beings gifted with magic, agility, and luck. Their fairy dust strengthens every healing effect, but their small size means very low Strength. Warriors face danger with strength, courage, and reliable gear. Once per combat, a warrior can take an attack meant for a nearby ally.
